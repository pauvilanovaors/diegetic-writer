# Before and After

These examples are quality references, not text to paste into unrelated outputs. Generate fresh wording for each request.

## Example 1: Decoration Versus Institutional Context

Request: ask a colleague for a report as an Imperial officer in Star Wars.

Weak transformation:

> Hello, fellow Imperial officer. Please send the quarterly fleet report through the HoloNet immediately. May the Force be with you.

Why it fails: forced address, decorative technology, an inappropriate stock closing, and no change to the working relationship.

Improved transformation:

> Lieutenant,

> The quarterly fleet report remains absent from the sector operations record. Transmit the completed document to my datapad by 1600 hours and identify any unresolved discrepancies before submission.

> Commander Vey

Why it improves: rank, records, deadline, channel, and accountability create the context. The invented name and exact deadline would be disclosed as creative additions if provenance were requested.

## Example 2: Practical Water Discipline

Request: postpone dinner with a friend in Dune as a Fremen.

Weak transformation:

> By the spice and the sacred desert, we must delay our feast beneath the moon of Arrakis.

Improved transformation:

> The northern run took longer than expected, and I cannot return to the sietch before nightfall. Keep the evening water share sealed; I will come tomorrow at dusk.

Why it improves: travel, return time, water care, and a revised meeting make the setting practical rather than ornamental. Details are creative additions serving the transformation.

## Example 3: Corporate Threat

Request: demand overdue documentation as a Cyberpunk 2077 corporate executive.

Weak transformation:

> Send the docs now, choom, or I'll flatline you with my Arasaka cyberware.

Improved transformation:

> Your project file is still incomplete. Submit the outstanding documentation before today's compliance review. Continued omission will be recorded as a performance and contractual issue, with access privileges reassessed accordingly.

Why it improves: employment, compliance, contracts, and access provide credible executive leverage without random slang or violence.
