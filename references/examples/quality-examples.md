# Quality Examples

Original examples only; do not reuse these names, dates, or sentences automatically.

## Immersion Comparison

Objective: request a fleet report from a colleague in Star Wars.

### Level 1 — Inspired

> Hi Mara, could you send me the quarterly fleet report by tomorrow? Command is asking for the latest figures, so I want to check them before our review.

### Level 2 — Immersive

> Officer Mara,

> Command has requested the quarterly fleet figures. Please transmit the report by tomorrow at 1600 so I can review any discrepancies before our sector briefing.

> Officer Ren

### Level 3 — In-Universe

> Lieutenant Mara,

> The quarterly fleet report has not yet been entered into the sector operations record. Transmit the completed file to my datapad by 1600 hours and append an explanation for any unresolved losses before the command review.

> Commander Ren, Sector Operations

### Level 4 — Deep Immersion

> Lieutenant Mara,

> Command's review will proceed with or without a complete accounting. Your report is the only missing record in this sector's quarterly fleet submission. Deliver the verified file to my datapad by 1600 hours. Any vessel whose loss cannot be reconciled will be named for further inquiry, with its supervising officer called to account.

> Commander Ren
> Sector Operations — Clearance Review Pending

The progression changes medium, hierarchy, accountability, and atmosphere; it does not merely add more fictional nouns. Creative details serve the example and are not canon.

## Perspective Comparison

Objective: arrange an urgent planning meeting.

Imperial officer:

> Captain, report to Command Briefing Room Three at 0900. Bring the revised convoy disposition and be prepared to account for the unresolved escort gap.

Rebel operative:

> We need a planning session at 0900. Bring what you have on the convoy and the escort gap; we will compare routes, fill the intelligence gaps, and leave with assignments everyone can carry out.

The contrast concerns command versus coordinated problem-solving, not just different faction labels.

## Tone Comparison

Diplomatic Bene Gesserit invitation:

> Your presence at tomorrow's house conference would allow several uncertainties to be addressed before positions harden into commitments. We shall receive you at the second hour after sunset. The discussion will concern safe conduct and the terms on which it might continue.

Diplomatic fixer invitation:

> Tomorrow's meeting is a chance to settle terms before both sides spend money solving the problem less politely. Second hour after sunset, neutral room, two guests each. Confirm attendance and I will hold the booking.

Both are diplomatic; their leverage, social ritual, and economic assumptions differ.

## Language Preservation

Spanish request objective: ask a friend for tomorrow's report in Star Wars.

> ¿Me pasas mañana el informe de carga? Tengo que revisar las rutas antes de salir del hangar.

The Spanish sentence remains natural while the relevant setting details remain concise. Do not translate established names mechanically or add an English explanation.
