# Cyberpunk 2077 Terminology

## Corporate and Civic

| Term | Meaning | Appropriate context |
|---|---|---|
| Megacorporation / corp | Corporation with major political, economic, and security power | Employment, markets, enforcement, liability |
| Arasaka | Major megacorporation with security and financial interests | Corporate hierarchy and security when selected |
| Militech | Major megacorporation and Arasaka rival | Military-industrial and corporate conflict contexts |
| NUSA | New United States of America | National politics and Militech relationships, not generic city government |
| NetWatch | Organization policing dangerous Net activity | Blackwall, rogue AI, Net security |
| NCPD | Night City Police Department, Night City's law enforcement | Security, law enforcement, corporate-city relations |
| Fixer | Broker connecting clients and mercenaries | Contracts, payment, intelligence, deniability |
| Merc / edgerunner | Independent contractor operating outside conventional employment | Gigs, crews, reputation, survival |
| Ripperdoc | Installer and maintainer of cyberware | Clinics, implants, repairs, debt |

## Technology and Street Terms

| Term | Meaning | Appropriate context |
|---|---|---|
| Cyberware / chrome | Cybernetic modification / chrome as style or substance | Body modification, status, maintenance |
| Netrunner | Specialist navigating and manipulating Net systems | Intrusion, ICE, digital reconnaissance |
| ICE | Intrusion countermeasures electronics | Network defense and intrusion risk |
| Blackwall | Barrier containing dangerous rogue-AI Net regions | Exceptional Net threats, not routine security |
| Braindance / BD | Recorded sensory experience | Media, investigation, entertainment, exploitation |
| Shard | Portable data-storage medium | Documents, recordings, exchanged data |
| Agent | Personal networked device/interface | Calls, messages, schedules, everyday coordination |
| Eddies | Eurodollars, common currency | Pay, costs, bribes, debt |
| Choom | Informal term for friend/associate | Trusted street-level speech, not formal corporate address |
| Preem / nova | Positive slang | Use sparingly and only in a fitting voice |

## Linguistic Guidance

"Corpo" is informal or dismissive for corporate personnel; "flatline" refers to killing/death in fitting contexts; "delta" can mean rapid departure. Slang should mark class, trust, and intent rather than appear once per sentence. Corporate communication favors nouns such as alignment, exposure, deliverables, continuity, liability, and optimization only when they conceal or clarify real institutional action.

## Interpretation for Writing

Invented project names, internal corporate offices, contract codes, and club venues are permissible creative additions when needed. They are not canonical and must be disclosed if the user requests provenance. Avoid borrowing memorable game dialogue as generic slang.
