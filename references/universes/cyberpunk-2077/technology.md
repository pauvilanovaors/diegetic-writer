# Cyberpunk 2077 Technology

| Technology | Communication-relevant use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Agent | Personal messaging, calls, schedules, coordination | Near-future personal device; adapt naturally rather than saying smartphone |
| Shard | Portable documents, recordings, programs, contracts | Not automatically encrypted or secure |
| Cyberware / chrome | Capability, status, maintenance, medical debt | Specific implant capabilities vary |
| Neural interface | Direct connection to systems | Do not imply unlimited access or knowledge |
| Netrunning | Intrusion, reconnaissance, control, countermeasures | Requires relevant access, skill, and risk |
| ICE | Network defense and danger to intruders | Not a generic password field |
| Blackwall | Containment boundary against rogue-AI regions | Not an ordinary corporate firewall |
| Braindance | Recorded sensory experience and editing | More than video; context and consent matter |
| Relic | Exceptional experimental biochip in the base-game plot | Not a routine data shard or office implant |
| Cyberpsychosis | Serious setting condition involving cyberware and psyche | Not a casual synonym for irritation or burnout |
| NCART | Night City public transit | Useful for ordinary travel logistics |
| Delamain | AI taxi service | Relevant transport option, not universal city infrastructure |

Technical references must change capability, cost, surveillance, danger, or logistics. Do not use cyberware as random decoration or introduce faster-than-light and supernatural concepts.
