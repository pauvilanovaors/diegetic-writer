# Cyberpunk 2077 Communication

## Corporate Channels

Use agent messages, corporate mail, encrypted shards, internal bulletins, compliance notices, performance reviews, and security advisories as appropriate. Corporate writing often converts harm into neutral process language: workforce reductions become optimization, coercion becomes compliance, and risk becomes exposure. Keep the real instruction legible beneath the euphemism.

## Street and Mercenary Channels

Use short agent messages, fixer briefs, dead-drop instructions, shard transfers, and direct conversation. Practical terms include payout, meeting point, gear, client limits, trace risk, and exit routes. Profanity and slang scale with relationship and stress; they are not mandatory decoration.

## Netrunning Communication

Separate physical location from Net intrusion, identify ICE and trace risk, and distinguish observed data from inference. Netrunners can be terse and technical with teammates but need not speak in unexplained jargon to clients. The Blackwall and rogue AI are exceptional risks, not synonyms for routine network trouble.

## Hierarchy and Leverage

Corporate executives pressure through employment, contracts, reputation, legal exposure, security, and resource access. Fixers pressure through clients, payout, information, and future gigs. Mercenaries negotiate capability and risk. Gang communication depends on selected territory and culture; avoid generic threats unsupported by the sender's position.

## Greetings and Closings

Corporate address uses roles, names, departments, and sanitized formulas. Street address uses chosen names, handles, and relationship-specific familiarity; "choom" requires suitable informality. Closings can request confirmation, payment, secure deletion, or check-in. Do not append a catchphrase to every message.

## Media and Advertising

Corporate ads sell aspiration, security, status, bodies, and belonging while hiding costs in conditions. Recruitment ads should identify role, promised benefit, actual requirements, and a response action. News can be sensational, corporate-aligned, or street-level; specify the outlet perspective rather than assuming objective narration.
