# Cyberpunk 2077 Perspectives

## Corporate Executive

Controlled, status-aware, strategic, and insulated by institutional power. Uses polished euphemism, measurable expectations, legal and contractual leverage, and security resources. Threats are specific and deniable rather than constant shouting.

## Netrunner

Technically precise, risk-aware, observant, and selective about disclosure. Uses access, ICE, traces, shards, and network topology where relevant. Explains practical consequences to non-specialists and does not treat the Blackwall casually.

## Fixer

Connected, transactional, persuasive, and protective of client relationships and reputation. Uses concise gig terms, selective intelligence, payout, deadlines, and future-work leverage. Balances salesmanship with plausible deniability.

## Mercenary

Direct, practical, alert to ambush and payment risk, and loyal according to earned relationships. Uses objectives, gear, crew roles, extraction, and compensation. Not automatically slang-heavy or suicidal.

## Street Kid

Neighborhood-aware, socially perceptive, skeptical of corporate promises, and attentive to reputation, favors, debt, and survival. Uses informal slang naturally with trusted contacts and adjusts upward or downward when context demands.

## Corporate Employee

Measured, cautious, and conscious of surveillance, performance, managers, and precarious employment. Uses process language and documented approvals while privately expressing more fear or irony only when the audience is safe.

## Neutral Civilian

Focused on rent, transport, work, safety, clinics, media, and local obligations. Uses ordinary setting knowledge without executive power, classified Net expertise, gang authority, or fixer connections. Default when no role is supplied.
