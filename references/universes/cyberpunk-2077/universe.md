# Universe: Cyberpunk 2077

## Overview

A dystopian near-future setting centered on Night City, where megacorporations, gangs, mercenaries, netrunners, media, and fragmented public authorities compete for power. Cybernetic modification and digital networks shape work, status, identity, and survival. Default period and continuity: the 2077 base game in Night City, without assuming Phantom Liberty-specific access or outcomes.

## Historical Context

- Corporate power expanded as public institutions weakened; Arasaka and Militech are major rival megacorporations with a history of armed corporate conflict.
- The Fourth Corporate War and the 2023 Arasaka Tower incident are historical background, not events every routine document needs to mention.
- The Blackwall separates the usable Net from dangerous rogue-AI territory; NetWatch polices relevant threats.
- Night City exists within a fragmented political order involving local government, corporations, the NUSA, and independent interests. Avoid importing real-world 2020s institutional assumptions unchanged.

## Political Structure

Megacorporations function as economic, security, and political powers. Night City's municipal institutions operate under severe corporate influence. Fixers broker illegal or deniable work; mercenaries execute contracts; gangs control neighborhoods and markets; NetWatch monitors dangerous Net activity. Nomad groups and communities maintain different forms of loyalty and exchange outside corporate hierarchies.

## Society and Culture

Status is performed through chrome, brands, vehicles, reputation, and access. Violence and precarity coexist with marketing and aspirational language. Corporate speech sanitizes exploitation; street speech can be direct, profane, ironic, and reputation-focused. Neighborhood, class, gang affiliation, and corporate employment affect trust. Do not make every speaker slang-heavy or every document maximally obscene.

## Technology

Cyberware, neural interfaces, braindance, netrunning, ICE, shards, agents, vehicles, and weapon systems can affect everyday communication. The Net is not equivalent to a harmless contemporary internet. Cyberpsychosis is a serious setting concept, not a generic insult for stress. The Relic is exceptional plot-relevant technology, not an ordinary storage device.

## Geography

Night City districts include City Center, Watson, Westbrook, Heywood, Santo Domingo, and Pacifica; the Badlands lie outside. Arasaka Tower, corporate campuses, clubs, ripperdoc clinics, megabuildings, and afterlife venues are useful only when relevant. NCART provides public transit. Avoid treating every location as equally safe or equally controlled by one corporation.

## Important Concepts

- Eddies (eurodollars) are common currency and practical leverage.
- Reputation, contracts, deniability, debt, and favors shape mercenary work.
- Corporate employment involves performance language, surveillance, hierarchy, and liability management.
- Chrome can signify utility, fashion, status, dependency, or identity.
- Braindance involves recorded sensory experience, not merely ordinary video.

## Canonical Boundaries

Do not introduce magic, faster-than-light travel, feudal noble houses, or institutions from unrelated universes. Do not make netrunning indistinguishable from ordinary web browsing, portray the Blackwall as a simple firewall, or use Arasaka and Militech as interchangeable brands. Avoid assuming Phantom Liberty outcomes or naming canonical characters unless requested.
