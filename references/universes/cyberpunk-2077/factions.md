# Cyberpunk 2077 Factions

## Arasaka

Globally powerful megacorporation associated with security, banking, and corporate influence. Internal communication is hierarchical, polished, risk-conscious, and attentive to loyalty, reputation, and liability. External threats use contracts, exposure, security, market power, or legal instruments before cartoonish violence.

Relationships: rivalry with Militech; entanglement with Night City authorities, clients, contractors, and criminal networks; internal loyalty does not eliminate factional competition.

## Militech

Megacorporation with armaments, security, and close NUSA associations. Communication can be forceful, operational, procurement-focused, and competitive. Do not use it as an interchangeable synonym for Arasaka or as the default employer in every request.

## NetWatch

Organization focused on Net security, rogue AI, and Blackwall-related threats. Communication emphasizes containment, traces, intrusion evidence, operational secrecy, and technical risk. It is not a general-purpose city police department.

## Fixers and Mercenary Networks

Fixers control client relationships, intelligence, job terms, and reputation; mercenaries supply capabilities and accept calculated risk. Messages emphasize objective, location, payout, expenses, expectations, leverage, and deniability. Trust varies by history; do not make every fixer sentimental or every mercenary blindly loyal.

## Gangs and Local Communities

Gangs combine territory, economy, identity, and coercion with distinct cultures. Avoid assigning a generic gang voice without selecting a group and context. Local communities and nomad groups can prioritize kinship, mutual aid, independence, and reputation over corporate advancement.

## Municipal and Corporate Authorities

Public services operate under resource constraints and corporate influence. Corporate departments manage risk through procedure, metrics, surveillance, and plausible deniability. A manager's authority derives from role and employer, not from generic dystopian omnipotence.
