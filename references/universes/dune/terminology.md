# Dune Terminology

## Power and Institutions

| Term | Meaning | Appropriate context |
|---|---|---|
| Padishah Emperor | Sovereign of the Corrino Imperium | Imperial court and high politics |
| Landsraad | Assembly/body of Great Houses | Noble political balance and formal disputes |
| Great House | Major noble house with fiefs and interests | Dynastic administration and diplomacy |
| CHOAM | Central commercial organization tying wealth to noble interests | Trade, profits, directorships, economic leverage |
| Spacing Guild | Monopoly holder of interstellar transport | Navigation, shipping, embargo, transport dependency |
| Bene Gesserit | Sisterhood with long-term political and genetic aims | Trained observation, diplomacy, bloodlines, influence |
| Mentat | Human trained for advanced computation and analysis | Intelligence and planning, not generic clerk |
| Sardaukar | Elite Imperial military force | Imperial coercion and military context |

## Arrakis and Fremen

| Term | Meaning | Appropriate context |
|---|---|---|
| Arrakis | Desert planet and sole spice source | Planetary administration and survival |
| Spice melange | Valuable substance essential to Guild navigation | Economics, logistics, addiction, strategy |
| Fremen | Native desert communities of Arrakis | Sietch society, ecology, resistance, water customs |
| Sietch | Fremen community and its place | Community governance and hospitality |
| Stillsuit | Suit preserving body moisture | Desert travel and water discipline |
| Water rings | Tokens representing water obligations/value | Fremen obligations where context supports them |
| Windtrap | Installation for collecting atmospheric moisture | Settlement ecology and water infrastructure |

## Technology and Titles

Ornithopters suit desert transport and spice operations; heighliners suit Guild interstellar transport; shields and lasguns require careful tactical treatment. Duke, Baron, Reverend Mother, naib, and Sayyadina have distinct social contexts. Do not distribute titles decoratively or treat “Muad'Dib” as a generic Fremen greeting.

## Interpretation for Writing

Formal court communications can plausibly use seals, witnesses, house offices, and carefully witnessed commitments. Fremen communications can use practical water accounting and sietch obligations. Specific invented offices, formulas, or depots are creative additions, not established canon.
