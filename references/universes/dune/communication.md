# Dune Communication

## Court and House Communication

Use precise titles, formal address, seals or witnesses where plausible, and carefully bounded commitments. Political meaning often sits in omission, sequencing, and what each party declines to promise. Routine administration should remain concrete about supplies, personnel, dates, and authority rather than becoming indiscriminate intrigue.

## Bene Gesserit Communication

Favor economical phrasing, observation, calibrated emphasis, questions that expose motive, and instructions with layered purposes. Do not announce secret breeding or religious programs to outsiders. A threatening message may identify behavioral consequences or political exposure instead of physical violence.

## Fremen Communication

Anchor obligations in sietch, water, shelter, guides, routes, weather, spice fields, and communal trust. Respectful address depends on relationship and local custom. Water references must alter the practical or social meaning; avoid decorative desert metaphors in offworld corporate messages.

## Guild and Commercial Communication

Use terms of carriage, routes, guarantees, fees, cargo priority, risk, and continuity. Guild speakers avoid unnecessary dynastic loyalty. CHOAM-influenced writers translate political changes into ownership, returns, access, and exposure.

## Greetings and Closings

Use house, title, rank, or community role as appropriate. Formal commitments may be witnessed or sealed; personal messages need no invented ritual. Closings can reaffirm obligation, safe passage, water responsibility, or house service when relevant. Do not borrow memorable passages from the novel as stock formulas.

## Conflict and Security

Espionage, assassination, sabotage, and surveillance affect what is written, entrusted to couriers, or left implicit. A secure report may separate verified observations from inference. Do not assume electronic encryption or modern networks; use human couriers, controlled records, and established institutions instead.
