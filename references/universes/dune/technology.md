# Dune Technology

| Technology | Communication-relevant use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Spice melange | Cargo value, supply, Guild leverage, exposure, addiction | Never reduce it to an interchangeable luxury good |
| Guild heighliner | Interstellar transport and scheduling | Guild-controlled; not a noble family's private taxi |
| Ornithopter | Desert reconnaissance, rescue, spice operations | Weather and worms affect operations |
| Stillsuit | Desert survival and travel planning | Requires correct use and maintenance |
| Windtrap | Settlement water infrastructure | Relevant to ecology and water budgets |
| Personal shield | Defense and tactical assumptions | Slow objects can penetrate; context matters |
| Lasgun | Directed-energy weapon | Shield interaction can be catastrophic; avoid casual pairing |
| Suspensor | Countergravity support and transport | Use only where physically relevant |
| Mentat | Human analysis and computation | Not a machine or an infallible oracle |
| Thinking machines | Prohibited historical category | Do not introduce ordinary computers or AI workflows |

Technology should change transport, survival, analysis, or risk. Do not use Holtzman terminology as generic decoration or invent precise technical specifications unsupported by the sources.
