# Universe: Dune

## Overview

A feudal interstellar civilization in which noble houses, the Padishah Emperor, the Spacing Guild, CHOAM, and the Bene Gesserit compete for power. Spice melange enables Guild navigation and shapes economics and politics. Arrakis is its only source and the home of the Fremen. Default period: the late Corrino Imperium during Frank Herbert's first novel, before Paul's accession.

## Historical Context

- The Butlerian Jihad led to the prohibition of thinking machines; trained humans and specialized institutions perform many functions instead.
- Great Houses belong to the Landsraad under the Padishah Emperor, while the Guild and CHOAM constrain political action through transport and commerce.
- House Atreides receives Arrakis from House Harkonnen control during the default-period crisis; their rivalry and Imperial/Guild interests shape communication.
- Later events under Paul Muad'Dib, the God Emperor, or later Bene Gesserit eras require an explicit period and must not be mixed into the default.

## Political Structure

The Emperor balances Imperial power against the Landsraad and relies on elite Sardaukar forces. Houses rule fiefs and hold economic interests through CHOAM. The Spacing Guild monopolizes safe interstellar transport. The Bene Gesserit exercise long-term influence through bloodlines, diplomacy, training, and planted religious systems. Fremen communities organize through sietches and leaders rather than Imperial corporate departments.

## Society and Culture

Court communication favors titles, layered intent, obligation, and controlled disclosure. Water discipline dominates Fremen life on Arrakis; waste, grief, hospitality, and property can carry meanings unlike those of offworld societies. Religion, prophecy, ecology, and political expedience intersect. Do not turn every speaker into a mystic or every practical message into a prophecy.

## Technology

Ornithopters, suspensors, personal shields, lasguns, stillsuits, windtraps, and Guild heighliners matter when they affect logistics or risk. Shield/lasgun interaction is catastrophic and must not be treated as routine weapons use. Computers and artificial intelligence in the contemporary sense are not ordinary office tools; human specialists such as Mentats may be relevant.

## Geography

Arrakis is the desert world containing Arrakeen, Carthag, sietch communities, the deep desert, and spice fields. Caladan is the Atreides homeworld; Giedi Prime is associated with House Harkonnen; Kaitain is the Imperial seat. Salusa Secundus has a specific Imperial history and should not be casually renamed or treated as a generic resort.

## Important Concepts

Spice determines transport, wealth, addiction, and strategy. Water is material survival and cultural meaning on Arrakis. Ecology connects Fremen hopes to planetary transformation. The Bene Gesserit breeding program and Missionaria Protectiva involve long-term manipulation, not casual conversation topics for outsiders. Prescience is exceptional and should not be assigned to ordinary speakers.

## Canonical Boundaries

Do not introduce thinking machines, contemporary corporations, smartphones, or conventional AI administration. Do not portray spice as a generic recreational drug without strategic significance, Fremen as offworld courtiers, or the Guild as an ordinary passenger airline. Do not give House Atreides Harkonnen cruelty or vice versa merely for tone. Avoid later-era institutions and named canonical characters unless requested.
