# Dune Factions

## House Atreides

A Great House associated with Caladan, disciplined command, loyalty, and a comparatively reciprocal bond with its people. Communication is controlled, honorable, strategic, and attentive to morale and obligations. Do not confuse honor with naivety: Atreides writers still assess espionage, logistics, and assassination risks.

Relationships: bitter rivalry with House Harkonnen; obligation to the Emperor; dependence on Guild transport; cautious engagement with Fremen and local networks on Arrakis.

## House Harkonnen

A Great House associated with Giedi Prime, extractive rule, coercion, and ruthless political ambition. Communication can be exploitative, suspicious, transactional, and threatening through actual control of resources or force. Avoid random sadism in routine messages; cruelty serves power and profit.

Relationships: rivals House Atreides; holds prior interests on Arrakis; navigates Imperial and Guild constraints while pursuing advantage.

## Bene Gesserit

A sisterhood using trained observation, controlled speech, diplomacy, breeding plans, and religious influence. Communication is precise, indirect when useful, and attentive to hidden motives and generational stakes. The Voice and Truthsaying are specialized capabilities, not decorative terms for ordinary persuasion.

Relationships: embedded across houses and the Imperial court while pursuing aims not identical to any employer's public goals.

## Fremen

Desert communities shaped by sietch loyalty, water discipline, ecological aims, and resistance to offworld exploitation. Communication is practical, communal, symbolically attentive to water, and direct when survival is at stake. Do not reduce Fremen voice to constant prophecy or archaic ornament.

Relationships: wary of offworld powers; internal obligations and leadership matter; alliances depend on trust, shared interest, and demonstrated respect.

## Spacing Guild

An economically indispensable organization controlling safe interstellar transport through Navigators and spice. Communication is cautious, transactional, strategically detached, and focused on routes, guarantees, fees, and stability. The Guild is not a noble house or a public civil service.

## Imperial Court and CHOAM

Imperial communication balances majesty, legal form, factional management, and concealed coercion. CHOAM communication emphasizes shares, profit, directorships, and economic leverage. Neither is synonymous with House Atreides, Harkonnen, or the Guild.
