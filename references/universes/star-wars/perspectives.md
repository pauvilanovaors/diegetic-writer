# Star Wars Perspectives

## Imperial Officer

Disciplined, authoritative, hierarchical, and alert to accountability. Uses formal, concise commands, explicit deadlines, authorization, reporting lines, and consequences within actual rank. To a superior, becomes controlled and justificatory rather than domineering.

## Rebel Operative

Pragmatic, collaborative, mission-oriented, and secrecy-aware. Uses verified intelligence, clear roles, operational urgency, fallback plans, and trust-appropriate warmth. Avoids Imperial administrative habits and unnecessary identifying details.

## Jedi

Measured, observant, compassionate, and attentive to fear, attachment, duty, and consequences. Prefers counsel and questions over bureaucratic commands. In the default period, does not assume a public Jedi hierarchy or widespread institutional authority.

## Sith

Controlled or intimidating, focused on power, ambition, leverage, secrecy, and domination. Uses implication and pressure rather than random rage. Not interchangeable with an ordinary Imperial officer.

## Smuggler

Self-reliant, transactional, evasive under scrutiny, and attentive to routes, cargo, debts, fuel, and payment. Can be wry or familiar with trusted contacts. Does not automatically share Rebel ideology or intelligence.

## Merchant

Concrete and reputation-conscious, emphasizing stock, manifests, delivery, customs, credit, and local market conditions. May cooperate with authorities or resistance for practical reasons without automatic ideological commitment.

## Neutral Civilian

Everyday concerns shaped by travel, shortages, local authority, droids, family, work, and safety. Uses limited setting knowledge and avoids classified operations, senior command authority, and specialized Force lore. Default when the user supplies no role.
