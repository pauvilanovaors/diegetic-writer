# Star Wars Technology

| Technology | Communication-relevant use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| HoloNet | Broad galactic communication and official distribution | Access, control, and local availability vary |
| Holographic transmission | Remote briefings, orders, recorded messages | Do not call it a modern video call at Levels 3–4 |
| Comlink | Direct personal or tactical communication | Useful for short operational exchanges |
| Hyperdrive / navicomputer | Travel duration, route disruption, ship repair | Do not use for unrelated data transfer |
| Droid / astromech | Translation, repair, navigation, data handling | Capabilities vary; do not assume omnipotence |
| Datapad | Portable reading, records, and administration | Plausible document medium for reports and inventories |
| Blaster / deflector shield | Combat and security context | Avoid decorative references in routine administration |
| Lightsaber / holocron | Force-related conflict, training, or archives | Not generic civilian tools |
| Death Star | Imperial coercive power at specific original-trilogy points | Check period and knowledge; not a casual office metaphor |

Technical detail should alter logistics, risk, medium, or authority. If removing the device changes nothing, remove the device. Never use a technology from another universe as a synonym.
