# Universe: Star Wars

## Overview

A galaxy-spanning space opera combining advanced interstellar technology with the Force, an energy field accessed by Jedi and Sith. Communications range from highly centralized military administration to clandestine resistance cells, frontier trade, and spiritual counsel. Default period: the Galactic Civil War depicted in the original film trilogy.

## Historical Context

- The Galactic Republic preceded the Galactic Empire; the Clone Wars helped enable that transition.
- During the default period, Emperor Palpatine rules the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance opposes it.
- The Death Star's destruction at Yavin and the Emperor's defeat at Endor are pivotal original-trilogy events; place a document before or after them only when the requested period supports it.
- The New Republic, First Order, Old Republic, and High Republic belong to other contexts. Do not import their institutions without an explicit period request and suitable references.

## Political Structure

The Empire centralizes authority under the Emperor, military command, governors, Moffs, and sector administration. The Rebel Alliance is a distributed resistance combining military commands, cells, diplomats, intelligence, and local supporters. Criminal syndicates, planetary governments, corporations, and independent traders retain varying influence, especially away from Imperial centers.

## Society and Culture

Many species and cultures coexist, although Imperial rule favors human centralized authority and uses fear, surveillance, and military control. Rebel communication depends on trust, secrecy, and operational need. Frontier communities value self-reliance, debts, favors, and reputation. Jedi traditions emphasize discipline and service; Sith traditions emphasize power and domination, but neither worldview is a generic public greeting.

## Technology

Hyperspace travel, droids, holographic communication, the HoloNet, starships, blasters, and cybernetics are ordinary parts of life. Lightsabers and holocrons are specialized Force-related references, not generic office vocabulary. Use the communication medium only when it improves the document's plausibility.

## Geography

Coruscant is the Imperial political center. The Outer Rim includes remote worlds such as Tatooine, where central authority and infrastructure may be weaker. Yavin IV and Hoth are associated with Rebel bases at specific times; avoid treating them as permanent headquarters. Sectors, systems, planets, and hyperspace routes provide useful administrative geography.

## Important Concepts

- The Force influences Jedi and Sith worldviews but is not a universal metaphor for routine work.
- Imperial order emphasizes obedience, control, rank, and ideological conformity.
- Rebellion emphasizes hope, sacrifice, secrecy, and coordinated action.
- Smugglers and merchants may frame obligations through cargo, routes, payment, debt, and risk.

## Canonical Boundaries

Do not call the Rebel Alliance an Imperial department or portray Imperial officers as serving the New Republic in the default period. Do not present Jedi as an Imperial branch, Sith as publicly interchangeable with ordinary officers, or droids as biological people. Do not use modern email, internet, smartphones, or contemporary corporations at Levels 3–4; use plausible transmissions and institutions instead. Avoid named canonical characters unless requested.
