# Star Wars Terminology

Use a term only when its speaker, institution, and context make it natural.

## Political and Military

| Term | Meaning | Appropriate context |
|---|---|---|
| Galactic Empire | Authoritarian government of the default period | Imperial authority, administration, loyalty |
| Imperial command | Relevant military or administrative superior authority | Routing orders and approvals; avoid vague repetition |
| Moff / Grand Moff | Imperial regional governor / senior governor | High-level governance, not routine team management |
| Sector / system | Regional / stellar administrative geography | Logistics, jurisdiction, deployments |
| Stormtrooper | Imperial infantry | Security and military operations, not clerical staff |
| Rebel Alliance / Alliance | Organized opposition to Imperial rule | Rebel strategy, coordination, diplomacy |
| Cell | Clandestine resistance group | Compartmentalized Rebel operations |
| Intelligence | Information gathered for Rebel operations | Mission planning and verified reports |
| Jedi | Force-using tradition associated with discipline and service | Jedi perspective or explicit Force context |
| Sith | Force-using tradition centered on power and domination | Sith perspective or explicit dark-side context |

## Technology and Travel

| Term | Meaning | Appropriate context |
|---|---|---|
| HoloNet | Galactic communications network | Broad or official communications where access is plausible |
| Holographic transmission | Remote audiovisual/message communication | Briefings, commands, recorded messages |
| Hyperdrive | Starship faster-than-light propulsion | Travel and engineering, unrelated logistics only by clear analogy |
| Hyperspace route | Route used for faster-than-light travel | Travel plans, blockades, smuggling |
| Droid | Artificial person/machine with varied functions | Technical assistance, labor, translation |
| Astromech | Droid class used for repair and navigation | Ship maintenance and astrogation |
| Blaster | Common directed-energy weapon | Security and combat, not generic office threats |
| Lightsaber | Force-associated melee weapon | Jedi/Sith and combat contexts only |
| Holocron | Force-related information repository | Jedi/Sith lore, not routine files |

## Locations and Expressions

Coruscant suits central administration; Tatooine and the Outer Rim suit remote trade or evasion. Titles such as Commander, Captain, General, and Officer should match the addressed role. “May the Force be with you” is associated with hope, farewell, and support, especially among Jedi and Rebels; do not append it to every document or use it as an Imperial bureaucratic formula.

## Interpretation for Writing

Imperial communications may use routing codes, clearance labels, order references, and acknowledgment deadlines as reasonable administrative inventions. Rebel communications may use authentication phrases and compartmentalized routing. Identify such invented details as creative additions when provenance is requested.
