# Star Wars Communication

## Media and Formality

Use holographic transmissions, recorded messages, secure channels, military orders, administrative memoranda, ship-to-ship messages, and HoloNet distribution when appropriate. A modern email can become a routed transmission at Levels 3–4 while retaining greeting/context/request/closing logic. Secure Rebel messages minimize identifying information; Imperial records often emphasize traceability.

## Hierarchy

Imperial writers address superiors with appropriate rank and controlled deference, peers with concise coordination, and subordinates with explicit instructions and accountability. Rebel writers still respect command but can emphasize shared purpose, field judgment, and necessity. Independent operators use reputation, leverage, and mutual interest rather than automatic deference.

## Greetings and Closings

Use role-appropriate names or titles rather than a universal stock greeting. Imperial closings may refer to authorization, command, or service when natural. Rebel closings may stress solidarity, safety, or hope; “May the Force be with you” must fit the relationship. Smugglers and merchants can close with payment, route, or rendezvous terms. Invented routing phrases are interpretations, not canonical quotations.

## Military Conventions

Mission communications prioritize objective, location, timing, chain of command, resources, risks, authentication, and acknowledgment. Imperial versions stress compliance and reporting; Rebel versions stress operational need, secrecy, and contingencies. Do not turn routine civilian messages into military orders unless the perspective warrants it.

## Commercial and Civilian Conventions

Cargo manifests, hyperspace routes, customs, docking arrangements, debts, and local shortages make everyday obligations concrete. Civilians may avoid political labels, especially under surveillance. Humor often comes from danger, scarcity, bureaucracy, or a speaker's self-interest rather than contemporary memes.

## Linguistic Patterns

Imperial prose is controlled, declarative, and procedural. Rebel prose is direct and purpose-driven, with warmth where trust exists. Jedi prose favors measured counsel; Sith prose favors leverage and dominance. Use these as tendencies, not caricatures.
