# Factual Consistency

Use three internal knowledge categories:

- Established: directly supported by the selected pack and its sources.
- Interpretation: a reasonable communication convention inferred from established institutions and culture.
- Creative addition: an invented person, place, unit, office, event, expression, or technical detail introduced for this output.

Creative additions are permitted when useful, but never describe them as established canon. When the user asks for reasoning or provenance, identify creative additions explicitly and distinguish interpretation from sourced fact.

## Pre-Return Checklist

- Universe: every institution, technology, expression, and location belongs to the selected universe.
- Period: governments, factions, titles, and technologies coexist in the selected period; use the pack default when none was requested.
- Faction and role: loyalty, hierarchy, resources, and knowledge match the sender.
- Terminology: each term has the meaning and context documented in the pack; no cross-universe borrowing.
- Modern language: no accidental contemporary idiom, platform, or institutional assumption at Levels 3–4 unless the world has an analogous convention.
- Objective: preserve requested facts, action, audience, tone, format, and language.
- Creativity: no invented canonical claim; disclose additions when explanation is requested.
- Copyright: prose is original, no substantial passage or distinctive sequence is reproduced, and no authorship by an original creator is claimed.
- Output: only the communication unless explanation or provenance was explicitly requested.

If a check fails, revise the draft and run the checklist again. Do not append the checklist to the result.
