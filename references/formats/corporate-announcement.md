# Corporate Announcement Format

Use an institution-to-audience structure with a clear change, rationale, and consequence.

## Components

1. Announcement title or subject.
2. Issuing authority and intended audience.
3. Decision or event.
4. Context or institutional rationale.
5. Impact on recipients, including dates and required actions.
6. Contact, authorization, or closing formula.

For a recruitment advertisement, lead with role or opportunity, then promised benefit, actual responsibilities, requirements, employer incentives, and response action. Institutional persuasion may conceal costs or emphasize ideology, but the practical offer must remain understandable.

Match the institution's communication power: a voluntary association, authoritarian administration, commercial employer, and local community should not announce the same way.
