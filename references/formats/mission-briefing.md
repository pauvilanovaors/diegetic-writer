# Mission Briefing Format

## Components

1. Mission title.
2. Classification or distribution limit when relevant.
3. Objective expressed as an achievable result.
4. Background and verified intelligence.
5. Operational details: location, timing, roles, sequence, and communication.
6. Resources, equipment, support, and constraints.
7. Risks, unknowns, and contingencies.
8. Instructions, rules of engagement, and extraction or completion condition.
9. Authorization and acknowledgment.

Do not invent surveillance certainty, equipment, or authority unsupported by the perspective. Keep facts, assumptions, and unknowns distinguishable. Scale the briefing to the mission: a small retrieval need not read like a war plan.
