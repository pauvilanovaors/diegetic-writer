# Corporate Memorandum Format

Use a traceable institutional structure for internal direction, official records, and reports.

## Components

1. Issuing office or authority.
2. Recipient or distribution.
3. Subject and reference where useful.
4. Effective date or reporting period when relevant.
5. Purpose and established context.
6. Decision, findings, procedure, or instruction.
7. Responsibilities, deadlines, and required acknowledgment.
8. Authorization or signature.

For an official document, emphasize authority, scope, operative provisions, and authentication. For a report, replace compulsory instructions with findings, evidence, analysis, limitations, and recommendations as appropriate. Separate observed facts from interpretation and creative additions internally.

At Levels 3–4, adapt routing and authentication to the selected world without erasing sender, subject, substance, and responsibility.
