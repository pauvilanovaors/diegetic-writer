# Historical Document Format

## Chronicle Components

1. Title, dating convention, or chronicler where useful.
2. Context establishing time, place, and actors.
3. Events in meaningful sequence.
4. Consequences and interpretation consistent with the chronicler's position.
5. Optional record, witness, or preservation formula.

## Newspaper Components

1. Headline communicating the central event.
2. Dateline or outlet context where plausible.
3. Lead answering the essential event and significance.
4. Supporting details in descending importance.
5. Attributed perspectives or evidence appropriate to the outlet.
6. Context and consequences.

A historical document has an authorial position. Signal bias through selection and framing rather than claiming omniscience. Do not present invented events as established canon when explanation is requested.
