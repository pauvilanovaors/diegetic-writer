# Email Format

Preserve the recognizable logic of an email while adapting its medium and labels to the universe.

## Components

1. Routing or subject when useful.
2. Greeting appropriate to sender, recipient, and hierarchy.
3. Context sufficient to understand the message.
4. Main message with the relevant facts.
5. Requested action, deadline, or response.
6. Closing and signature or authorization.

For a letter, emphasize physical or ceremonial address, date, body, closing, and signature rather than electronic routing. For an invitation, include event, audience, time, place, purpose, and response instructions; adapt formality to the relationship.

At Levels 1–2, contemporary labels may remain. At Levels 3–4, replace the delivery convention when the world supplies a plausible equivalent, but do not let invented routing detail obscure the message. Omit empty components rather than filling them with lore.
