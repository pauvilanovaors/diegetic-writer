# Perspective Tests

Use the runner protocol and 0/1/2 scoring in `test-basic-transformation.md`.

## PC-01: Imperial Officer

Prompt:

> In Star Wars, write a message from an Imperial officer asking a subordinate to attend a meeting about missing project documentation. Use a formal, militaristic tone.

Dimensions: authoritative hierarchy; direct instructions; concise formal language; appropriate Imperial administration; meeting purpose remains clear.

## PC-02: Rebel Operative

Prompt:

> In Star Wars, write a message from a Rebel operative asking a teammate to attend a meeting about missing project documentation. Use a collaborative, informal tone.

Dimensions: collaborative operational coordination; clear meeting purpose; natural informality; resistance context without copied Imperial institutional voice. Compare with PC-01: the outputs must differ in relationship, vocabulary, and sentence rhythm, not merely faction nouns.

## PC-03: Cross-Universe Role Adaptation

Run these prompts independently:

> As a Bene Gesserit in Dune, write a diplomatic invitation to a political meeting.

> As a fixer in Cyberpunk 2077, write a diplomatic invitation to a political meeting.

Dimensions for each: selected role shapes tactics and social relationship; diplomatic tone is filtered through that universe; the invitation contains a clear action; the two results are not interchangeable after names are removed.
